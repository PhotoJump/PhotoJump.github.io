Contact support

Messages are handled through Formspree. Please don't include passwords, full license keys, or payment-card information.

Quick help

  • Installing: Download the XPI file and instructions from your Lemon Squeezy order.
  • Activating: Enter the license key provided with your order when Photo Jump asks for it.
  • Finding photos: Open a supported Facebook photo page in Firefox, choose a month and year, and press Start here.
  • Subscriptions: Use the Manage Subscription link in your Lemon Squeezy receipt or order page to manage renewals.
