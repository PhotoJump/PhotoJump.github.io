Photo Jump Support

Help with installation, license activation, and finding older Facebook photos.

Quick answers

How do I install Photo Jump?

Open your Lemon Squeezy order receipt, download the signed XPI and included instructions, then open the XPI in desktop Firefox and approve the installation.

How do I activate my license?

Open a supported Facebook photo page in Firefox. When Photo Jump asks, enter the license key provided with your Lemon Squeezy order.

How do I find older photos?

On a supported Facebook photo collection, choose a month and year and press Start here.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Use Manage Subscription from your Lemon Squeezy order email or order portal. Check that your cancellation was confirmed there.

Does Photo Jump work on phones or other browsers?

The currently sold extension is for desktop Firefox. It does not work inside the Facebook mobile app.
