Privacy

Website: This static website is designed without advertising trackers or analytics scripts.

Payments: When paid checkout is enabled, payment and billing information will be handled by the checkout provider. This website will not directly store credit-card details.

Changes: This privacy notice may be updated before the commercial launch if the final billing or licensing system changes.
