Privacy — Photo Jump

Website

Photo Jump's static website does not intentionally include advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Hosting and browser providers may collect technical logs under their own policies.

Support requests

When you submit our support form, the name, email address, subject and message you provide are processed by Formspree and delivered to our support Gmail inbox. Copies or support alerts may be forwarded to another inbox used to manage Photo Jump support. We use the information to respond to support questions.

AI-assisted support

Some support requests are automatically classified or answered using Google's Gemini API. Relevant message text may be sent to Google after basic attempts to remove obvious personal identifiers. This filtering is not perfect, so please do not include sensitive, confidential or personal information in the message. Google's Gemini API free tier may use submitted content and responses to improve Google's products, and human reviewers may review it. Complicated questions may be handled manually.

Purchases and extension

Subscriptions and payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy. This website does not directly collect payment-card details. Photo Jump works inside desktop Firefox on supported Facebook photo pages and is an independent product, not affiliated with Meta or Facebook.

Contact

Use our support form for questions. Do not submit passwords, card numbers, full license keys, personal photos, or confidential legal material.

For more information about outside services, see the Formspree privacy policy and Gemini API terms.
