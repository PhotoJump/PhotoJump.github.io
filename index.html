FAQ

Common questions.

Why would this be useful for a teacher or classroom page?

Classroom pages can build up years of photos. Photo Jump is intended to make it easier to return to a particular month or school year when looking for older activities, events, projects, or memories.

What does Photo Jump do?

It helps Firefox users navigate supported Facebook photo collections by choosing a month and year instead of relying on long manual scrolling.

Which browser does it support?

Photo Jump is currently designed for Mozilla Firefox.

Does it work outside Facebook?

No. It is specifically designed for supported Facebook photo and media pages.

How does the 7-day trial work?

The planned launch setup gives full access for seven days. After the trial, the plan you choose renews monthly or yearly unless you cancel future renewal.

Is this made by Facebook or Meta?

No. Photo Jump is an independent browser extension. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Meta Platforms, Inc.
