Terms — Photo Jump for Firefox

Photo Jump is an independent desktop Firefox browser extension intended to make navigation through supported Facebook photo collections easier.

The extension depends on the behavior of Facebook pages, which may change. Future compatibility is not guaranteed.

Photo Jump offers monthly and yearly subscription options with a 7-day free trial where shown at checkout. The checkout page controls the actual price, trial, renewal, cancellation and payment terms. Manage your subscription through your Lemon Squeezy order or customer portal.
